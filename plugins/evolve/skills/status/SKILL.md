# evolve:status — where the search stands

Run `evolve board` (add `--json` if you need to compute), read `evolve/insights.md`, and report:

1. **The board** — the top candidates by fitness (ids, scores, modes), and whether the top scores are full-budget or proxy-only (proxy-only leaders are unconfirmed; say so). Ranking is measurement, not a target — the search doesn't crown anything. `board` reports ids, not code; to see a candidate's actual change without side effects, run `evolve apply --id <id> --dry` (prints the genome/diff; applies nothing).
2. **Budget** — generations and full evals spent vs configured caps, staleness (generations of search work since a new best fitness was recorded — a progress-recency readout, not a crowned target), and the noise floor. If `exhausted` is non-empty, the engine will refuse further sampling — present the options: raise the budget in `evolve/evolve.json`, stop and take what the archive holds, or change the search's shape (new axes, different operators mix). A project with no budget block (or `"budget": {"disabled": true}`, shown as a `COUNTS:` line) has no engine stop conditions — stopping is the owner's call; report spend, don't invent a limit.
3. **Signal quality** — the archived guardrail-failure rate (records with `fitness: null`, shown as board's per-inventor/per-operator `failed` counts); a high rate usually means the brief's contract section or the smoke gate needs sharpening. Guard and dedup rejections are session-local Reflexion feedback and are NOT in the archive, so don't look for them here. Report per-inventor and per-operator stats as numbers, without editorializing. Surface any `board` warnings (multi-environment or multi-commit selection) prominently — those mean the leaderboard is mixing incomparable scores.
4. **Validation state** — validity is per-candidate: every scored record already passed `eval.guardrails`, and retracted ids are out of selection in every partition (`evolve retract` is the verb; say so if a retraction is recent). Pruned ids are a different status — score still valid, candidate held out of selection because its traits are carried elsewhere (`board` prints the count; `doctor` re-audits every standing prune's coverage and warns when a later retraction thinned a pruned candidate's carriers — surface those warnings, the remedy is `evolve prune --id … --reinstate`). If a leading candidate is being taken downstream, report whether it has a one-shot `final`-split validation; if not, recommend that single report-only run before any external claim. If `board` shows **env offsets**, report whether environments are comparable or should be partitioned. There is no promotion or adoption state to report — shipping is an engineering act outside the search.
5. **Next step** — one concrete recommendation: another round, a recombination round, a noise re-measure (`evolve doctor --measure-noise` after any eval/harness change), or stop.

Keep it to the numbers the archive actually holds — no verdicts the records don't support.
